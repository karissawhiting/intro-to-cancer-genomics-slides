# GEMS Intro to Cancer Genomics and IMPACT Slides

Slides from "Introduction to Cancer Genomics & MSK-IMPACT" (GEMS Training, 2023-05-30), presented by Karissa Whiting (slide co-author: Esther Drill).

## Contents

- `GEMS Intro to Cancer Genomics  & IMPACT data 2023-06-01.pdf` — training slide deck

## Agenda

- Genomics: cancer genomics, precision oncology, genetic mutations, genomic sequencing
- MSK-IMPACT: MSK genomics, IMPACT data ({cbioportalR}), IMPACT analyses and visualizations ({gnomeR})

## Topics covered

- Cancer as a genetic disease, and the basics of precision oncology
- Targeted therapy success stories (e.g. Gleevec/BCR-ABL, Herceptin/HER2, Erlotinib/EGFR)
- Genetics review: germline vs. somatic mutations, causes and downstream effects
- Tumor DNA sequencing assays: broad (whole genome/exome) vs. targeted (gene panel) approaches
- MSK-IMPACT: goals, FDA authorization, panel versions (IM3–IM7) and gene counts
- OncoKB and its role in identifying oncogenicity and clinical actionability
- Data sharing via cBioPortal and AACR, and questions of interest for IMPACT data analysis
- Accessing IMPACT data with the `{cbioportalR}` R package (public vs. authenticated MSK access)
- How IMPACT data is organized in cBioPortal (sample IDs, mutation status, data format quirks)
- Visualization and analysis of IMPACT data with `{gnomeR}`
